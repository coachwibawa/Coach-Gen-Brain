Writing pipeline, with the tone layer

The current pipeline from the coach-gen-writer skill, with the recommendation of 19 Nov 2026 added: classic style as the book stance, a tone line per section, the tone bank, and the four dials as a check only.

  • You
  • Claude or a tool
  • Stored
  • New with the tone layer

1. The flow

The groups follow Flower and Hayes (1981): planning, translating, reviewing. You are the monitor: every gold step is yours.

2. What shapes each step

What feeds the writing. The green inputs are new. Nothing here is a checklist Claude drafts from.

3. How your marks feed back

Each epoch teaches the next one. The edit pairs into the tone bank are new.
