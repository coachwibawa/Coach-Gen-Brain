The six labels: one language for writing and markup

One language for both of us, used at every point of the loop: before drafting, in the rules, in your markup, and in what gets stored.

Built from two models: Richards (what is said, feeling, tone, intention) and Flower and Hayes (planning, putting into words, reviewing). The examples are your own comments, word for word.

Cards 1 to 6 are proposed definitions. Cards 13 to 17 are your calls, with no pick from Claude.
