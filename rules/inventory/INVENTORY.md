## How to read it

- **Tiers.** Tier 0 and Tier 1 fail a draft. Tier 2 never does (his words, 5 Nov: "some t2 edits, but pass t1 t0").
  - **Tier 0:** truth and hard mechanics. Never in a delivered draft. Checked by script, or against the material.
  - **Tier 1:** core craft. Judged on the whole passage, one checker per rule (step 12).
  - **Tier 2:** polish. The writer applies it. Lint fixes the mechanical ones silently.
- **Outputs:** A article, L letter, C chapter, K key moments, S case study. `social`, `reel`, `brand`, `content` are out of scope for the five cards.
- **Check:** `mech` script (cg_lint, step 11), `checker` one-rule checker (step 12), `judge` the writer's judgment, never fails.
- **Action:** `keep` this row is the main line of its target. `merge` folded into the target. `cut` dropped (duplicate, stale, superseded, or history). `move` goes to a home outside the rulebook. `out` stays in its own doc, untouched. `open` waits on his call.
- **Target:** a rule in the proposed rulebook (T0.x, T1.x, T2.x), a card, or a destination. The wording of each target is written in step 8.
- **Loc:** line numbers in the archived copy, so step 8 can copy exact text by script.
