## Cutting What Still Matters

Killing hurts, and it will hurt you too. But what you truly need, you can go back to with a clear mind and restart from a fresh seed, the way I did. And this time, there will be room in your month to give it 100%.

I was a member of BNI (a business networking group) and of an entrepreneur community. On top of that came a few communities where I simply hung out, my friends' group chats, and the Inside the Introvert Community.

Not one of these came to me ready-made. I had grown each of them from zero to where it was. I did not start my Japanese fluent, and now that it had finally borne fruit, it felt really bad to neglect it.
