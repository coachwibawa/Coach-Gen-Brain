# 2. Locked — The 5 Laws

*Sourcing note: the Law wording (column 2) is confirmed, word for word, in this thread. The quotes (column 3) were never explicitly re-paired with the new Law names in this thread — they**'**re carried over from the original numbering on the assumption that each Law**'**s underlying concept didn**'**t change, only its wording. Worth a quick yes/no confirmation before print.*

| **#** | **Law (final wording)** | **Quote** | **Chapter concept** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reset My Course | Seneca: "If a man knows not which port he sails to, no wind is favorable." | Courage. Most people run hard in the wrong direction to avoid failure or protect a hidden reward (safety, freedom, comfort). Turning around takes courage. |
| 2 | Reframe Past Regret | Carl Jung: "I am not what happened to me, I am what I choose to become." | Past. Regret is the word most people already associate with this stage (paired with a felt sense of failure) — kept deliberately, not softened. Don't fix the past — extract its wisdom and choose your current identity. |
| 3 | Resolve Present Friction | Marcus Aurelius: "The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way." | Present. Obstacles trigger creative problem-solving. Confronting the roadblock converts friction into momentum. |
| 4 | Reap Future Habits | Aristotle: "We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit." | Future. "Reap" was chosen over "Reinforce"/"Replant" specifically to convey ease — habits arrive as the harvest of Laws 1-3, not fresh labor. Differentiator: habits alone fail without Laws 1, 2, 3, 5 in place first. |
| 5 | Relax To Results | Lao Tzu: "Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished." | Results. "To" (not "Into") chosen deliberately — relax, and as a result of the system, results follow. Coach Gen's own image: riding a Shinkansen or Deutsche Bahn train, you relax because you know you'll get to your destination on time — unlike a stressful, loud departure or landing. The one passive Law by design: Claude proposed framing it as "four acts of building, one act of trusting," which Coach Gen confirmed. |

Sequence rule: the Laws are sequential and load-bearing, not pick-and-choose. Skipping one doesn't make the next impossible — it makes it fail to hold (burnout, backslide, roughly 3 months).

**Unifying unpacking line (title/law layer): **"You do not fail to make lasting change. You succeed at staying who you are." — locked wording.

*Note: earlier diagnostic-register variants (e.g. **"**You don**'**t fail to maintain your new self, you succeed in maintaining your current self**"**) were working drafts used to prove the law holds up when unpacked — not intended for print. In session/content, Coach Gen unpacks the law live, in his own words, each time.*

# 3. Voice Registers — When to Use Which

*Sourcing note: this three-register breakdown is Claude**'**s analysis, offered mid-conversation and then endorsed by Coach Gen (**"**consistency is really important though, so it**'**s better to have the title be law form, but the content be in other forms**"**). The framework is confirmed; the exact category names below are Claude**'**s phrasing, not Coach Gen**'**s.*

| **Register** | **What it does** | **Best used for** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Law form | States a universal truth, third-person or generalized "you." No direct address, no reassurance — reads like a fact, not a pitch. | Framework name, book title, chapter openers, cold ad headlines, book cover, front matter — any first touch with a stranger. |
| Diagnostic / advice form | Speaks directly to one reader about their specific pattern. Reassures and names what's happening to them. | Inside a chapter, session content, warm follow-ups, DMs after engagement — once the reader already suspects it's about them. |
| Command form | Tells the reader what to do. The 5 Laws themselves. | Framework structure, workbook headers, program steps. |

Working rule: title layer stays in law form; content layer can shift into diagnostic or command form. Consistency matters because ad language becomes course/curriculum language — whatever appears in an ad, Coach Gen is committed to teaching.

# 5. Content Formats

- Myth vs. Reality — expose false beliefs about capability, highlight innate resourcefulness

- Diagnostic Questions — sharp questions that force self-reflection, locate the bottleneck

- Micro Tools — 5-minute actionable exercises with immediate clarity

- Client Stories — real transformations illustrating a specific Law

- Identity Shifts — focus on who the reader becomes, not just what they do

Keeping the system visible while isolating one Law: anchor lines ("This is Law X of 5"), cause-and-effect bridges (skipping Law X breaks Law Y), visual breadcrumbs (dim/highlight all 5 on slides), sequence triggers (current Law as prerequisite for the next).
