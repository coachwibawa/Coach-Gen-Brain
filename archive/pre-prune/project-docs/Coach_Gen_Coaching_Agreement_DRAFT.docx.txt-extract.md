Description of Coaching: coaching is a partnership (an alliance, not a legal business partnership) between Coach and Client in a thought-provoking and creative process that inspires the Client to maximize personal and professional potential. It is designed to facilitate the development of personal, professional, or business goals and to develop and carry out a plan for achieving them, using the 5 Laws of Lasting Change framework.

**1) Coach-Client Relationship**

A. Coach agrees to maintain the ethics and standards of behavior established by the International Coaching Federation (ICF, coachingfederation.org/ethics). Client is encouraged to review the ICF Code of Ethics.

B. Client is solely responsible for creating and implementing his or her own physical, mental, and emotional well-being, decisions, choices, actions, and results arising from the coaching relationship. Coach is not and will not be liable or responsible for any actions or inaction, or for any direct or indirect result of any services provided by Coach. Client understands that coaching is not therapy and does not substitute for therapy if needed, and does not prevent, cure, or treat any mental disorder or medical disease.

C. Client may terminate or discontinue the coaching relationship at any time, subject to Section 9 (Termination).

D. Client acknowledges that coaching is a comprehensive process that may involve different areas of life, including work, finances, health, relationships, education, and recreation. Deciding how to handle these areas and implementing choices is exclusively the Client's responsibility.

E. Client acknowledges that coaching does not involve the diagnosis or treatment of mental disorders and is not a substitute for counseling, psychotherapy, psychoanalysis, mental health care, substance abuse treatment, or other professional advice by legal, medical, or other qualified professionals. It is the Client's responsibility to seek such independent professional guidance as needed. If Client is currently under the care of a mental health professional, Client is encouraged to inform that provider of the nature and extent of this coaching relationship.

F. Client agrees to communicate honestly, be open to feedback, and create the time and energy to participate fully in the program.

**2) Services**

The parties agree to engage in the **[30-Day Program / 90-Day Lifetime Change]**, consisting of **[4 / 13]** one-on-one coaching sessions conducted via Google Meet, each 60 minutes (up to 90 minutes for the first session or sessions requiring deeper work, at no additional charge), at a pace of approximately one session per two weeks.

During the active coaching period (Laws 1 through 4), Coach will be available to Client via WhatsApp with a same-day response during active coaching weeks. Following program completion, Client receives Aftercare: WhatsApp check-ins, a newsletter, and access to the WhatsApp inner circle community, in fulfillment of Law 5.

**5) Confidentiality**

This coaching relationship, and all information (documented or verbal) Client shares with Coach as part of this relationship, is bound by the principles of confidentiality set forth in the ICF Code of Ethics. The Coach-Client relationship is not a legally privileged relationship (unlike the medical and legal professions), and communications are not subject to the protection of any legally recognized privilege. Coach agrees not to disclose any information pertaining to Client without Client's written consent, and will not disclose Client's name as a reference without consent.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) was in Coach's possession before being furnished by Client; (b) is generally known to the public or in Client's industry; (c) is obtained by Coach from a third party without breach of any obligation to Client; (d) is independently developed by Coach without reference to Client's confidential information; (e) Coach is required by statute, subpoena, or court order to disclose; (f) is disclosed to Coach where Coach reasonably believes there is an imminent or likely risk of danger or harm to Client or others; or (g) involves illegal activity.

**7) Cancellation Policy**

Client agrees to notify Coach at least 24 hours in advance of a scheduled session to reschedule or cancel. Coach reserves the right to bill Client for a missed session given without such notice. Coach will attempt in good faith to reschedule a missed meeting.

**8) Record Retention Policy**

Client acknowledges that Coach maintains records (session notes, worksheet responses, and related documentation) digitally, and retains such records for not less than 3 years following program completion.

**9) Termination**

Either Client or Coach may terminate this Agreement at any time. If Client terminates before completing the full program, Client agrees to compensate Coach for all coaching services rendered through the effective date of termination; any remaining unused sessions are forfeited and non-refundable.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, Coach retains sole discretion to offer a partial or full refund of unused sessions on a case-by-case basis. Any such discretionary refund is a one-time accommodation and does not create an obligation, expectation, or precedent for any future termination, by this Client or any other.

This Section is separate from, and does not limit or expand, the guarantee described in Section 3, which applies only upon completion of the full program.

**10) Limited Liability**

Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Coach makes no guarantees, representations, or warranties of any kind, express or implied, with respect to the coaching services negotiated, agreed upon, and rendered. In no event shall Coach be liable to Client for any indirect, consequential, or special damages. Coach's entire liability under this Agreement, and Client's exclusive remedy, is limited to the amount actually paid by Client to Coach under this Agreement for all coaching services rendered through the termination date.

**11) Entire Agreement**

This document reflects the entire agreement between Coach and Client and supersedes all prior written and oral representations. This Agreement may not be amended, altered, or supplemented except in writing signed by both parties.

**12) Dispute Resolution**

If a dispute arises out of this Agreement that cannot be resolved by mutual consent, Client and Coach agree to attempt mediation in good faith for up to 30 days after notice is given. If the dispute is not resolved and legal action follows, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover attorney's fees and court costs from the other party, subject to Section 15.

**13) Severability**

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force. If a court finds a provision invalid or unenforceable but that limiting it would make it valid and enforceable, that provision shall be deemed written, construed, and enforced as so limited.

**14) Waiver**

The failure of either party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not be construed as a waiver of that party's right to subsequently enforce and compel strict compliance with every provision of this Agreement.
