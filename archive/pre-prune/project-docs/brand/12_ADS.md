# 12 Ads (Meta and LinkedIn)

Version 1.0, 30 Oct 2026. Load with 01_CORE.md, plus 10_VIDEO.md for video ads or 11_SOCIAL.md for image ads.

## 1. Build once, export three
Design one layout in 9:16, keep everything inside the 9:16 safe zone (y 270 to 1250, x 64 to 960), then export 4:5 and 1:1 crops of the same layout. LinkedIn single-image ads also get 1.91:1 (1200 x 628).

## 2. Rules
1. One message per ad. The landing page repeats the ad's headline word for word and uses the same color family.
2. Title line by targeting: corporate and HR audiences get "ICF Associate Certified Coach (ACC)". Individual audiences get "Certified Professional Coach (CPC)".
3. Blue family by default. Gold only when the ad sells the book or a speaking event.
4. Logo mark on every ad, inside the safe zone.
5. Headline in Montserrat 800, max 8 words. At most 20 words of text on the image.
6. Claims Gen can prove only. No fake countdowns or "only 2 spots left" unless true.
7. Meta personal attributes policy: never imply the viewer has a health or mental-health condition ("your anxiety", "your depression"). Speak to the pattern instead: "If you keep trying, but never finish, this is why."
8. Real photos of Gen or brand graphics. No AI-made people.

## 3. Naming files
`AD_<campaign>_<audience>_<format>_v<n>.png`, for example `AD_5Laws-diagnostic_corporate_9x16_v1.png`.

## 4. Checklist before delivery
1. Three formats from one layout.
2. Title line matches the targeting.
3. Headline matches the landing page.
4. No health or mental-health wording aimed at the viewer.
5. Mark present, text inside the safe zone.
