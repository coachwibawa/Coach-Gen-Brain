# Coach Gen website rules (for Antigravity)

Add this file to the rules of the coachwibawa.com workspace in Antigravity, so every change it makes follows the brand. Version 1.2, 30 Oct 2026. Source of truth: Coach Gen brand guide, modules 01_CORE and 13_WEB.

## Always
1. Load tokens.css (attached) and use its variables for every color, font, radius, and spacing value.
2. Fonts: Montserrat (weights 400, 600, 700, 800) and Libre Baskerville (400, 400 italic, 700) from Google Fonts. Remove any other font family from the project.
3. Blue family by default. The gold family appears only in a full-width section about the book or Lasting Change Letters.
4. Square corners: radius 0, or 4 px on buttons and form fields. No gradients, glows, or box shadows.
5. Max content width 1200 px, 12 columns, 24 px gutters, 24 px side padding on mobile. Section spacing 96 px desktop, 64 px mobile.
6. Header: the horizontal Coach Gen logo (blue), the menu, one button.
7. Every offer uses the same offer card: sub-brand mark, name, one-line promise, who it is for, one button, a photo slot on top.
8. First person "I" in all copy. Never "we", "our team", or "Coach Wibawa".
9. Page titles: "<Page name> | Coach Gen". Author: Genison Wibawa.
10. Every statistic shows its source next to it. Testimonials are real clients only.

## Before finishing any change
- Every menu link opens a real page (no 404).
- Only the two font families load (check the network tab).
- No gradient, glow, or shadow in the CSS.
- Text contrast passes: body text uses the approved pairs in tokens.json.

## Never
- Invent a new color, font, radius, or shadow.
- Add stock photos of people or AI-made people.
- Change the logo files.
