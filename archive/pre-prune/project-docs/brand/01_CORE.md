Rules:
1. One family per piece. Never gold and Aqua in one composition. A web page may hold one full-width gold section for the book.
2. The logo uses the piece's family.
3. No new colors. Lighter lines: a palette color at 20% (on light) or 25% (on dark). Secondary text: body color at 72%.
4. Approved text pairs (contrast checked 30 Oct 2026): Mist/Abyss 17.0, Aqua/Abyss 10.1, Abyss on Aqua 10.1, White on Deep Water 9.8, Deep Water on Mist 8.8, Ivory/Charcoal 12.7, Gold on Charcoal 7.2, Charcoal on Gold 7.2. Deep Gold on Ivory 3.2 is for headings 24 px and up only.

6. ADDED (30 Oct 2026): reserved series accents, not in use: #FFD400 yellow, #FF6A13 orange, #3FA9F5 blue. A future series may claim one with Gen's approval. A series accent never sits next to Aqua, and passes the contrast check first.

## 5. Type
- Montserrat: headings, labels, buttons, UI, captions, ad headlines. Weights: 800 captions and ad headlines, 700 headings, 600 labels and buttons, 400 short body.
- Libre Baskerville: long reading, quotes, the Thought-style Reel. 400, 400 italic, 700 for a pulled line.
  - ADDED (30 Oct 2026): the pair covers every surface (site, social, video, ads, documents). Libre Baskerville is also the headline font in Thought and Author modes (11_SOCIAL section 1b). Never in moving captions.
- Nothing else. Retired: Manrope, Oswald, Outfit, PT Serif, Bebas Neue, Anton, Geist.
- Sentence case for headings. Caps only for eyebrow labels (+0.12 em), buttons (+0.06 em) and video captions (+0.01 em).
- Minimum text on a 1080-wide canvas: 24 px.

## 6. Shape (from the logo)
- Square corners. Radius 0, or 4 px on buttons and form fields only.
- Straight rules only: 2 px on screen, 4 px on 1080 canvases, 0.5 pt in print.
- Everything on the grid. Base unit 8 px. Spacing steps 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128.
- No diagonals, gradients, glows, drop shadows, speed lines or squiggles.
- Motif: the maze line. A straight line with right-angle turns, stroke to gap 4 to 3. Used for dividers, frames, and the 5-step progress line (one turn per Law).

## 7. Logo (full rules in LOGO-USAGE.md)
- Shapes: stacked, horizontal, horizontal with title line, mark only, wordmark only, small mark, micro mark.
- Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height. Minimum: mark 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print.
- Never rotate, skew, outline, shadow, recolor outside the palette, or place on a busy photo without a solid panel.
