# 14 Documents, slides and print

Version 1.0, 30 Oct 2026. Load with 01_CORE.md.

## 1. A4 documents (worksheets, proposals, program documents)
- Margins 20 mm, 12-column grid.
- Headings Montserrat 700 (H1 22 pt, H2 16 pt, H3 12 pt). Body Montserrat 400 10.5 pt, line spacing 1.45. Long reading text may use Libre Baskerville 10.5 pt.
- Header: horizontal logo, 30 mm wide, top left. Footer: "Coach Gen | coachwibawa.com" and the page number, 8 pt.
- Family: blue for free worksheets and diagnostics. Gold for paid program documents (30-Day, 90-Day), proposals tied to speaking, and anything for the book.
- Title line by audience (01_CORE section 2). Corporate proposals use ICF ACC.

## 2. Sending files
Montserrat and Libre Baskerville are not installed on most clients' computers. Anything sent outside goes as PDF with fonts embedded. Send a .docx only when the person must edit it, and say the fonts may fall back.

## 3. Slides (16:9, 1920 x 1080)
- Title 64 px Montserrat 700, body 32 px minimum, max 30 words a slide.
- One idea per slide. Mark bottom right at 48 px on content slides.
- Blue family for teaching, gold for keynotes and book talks.

## 4. Invoices, receipts, contracts
Header from `CoachGen-Logo-Kit/08_Invoice-header`. Legal name PT Gapai Bangkit Wibawa. Black and white only, so they print cleanly.

## 5. Print
- CMYK PDF, 3 mm bleed, fonts outlined or embedded.
- Business card and name tag: finished in `CoachGen-Logo-Kit/09_Print`.
- Gold in print: use Deep Gold for text on light stock. Metallic gold foil only for the book and premium cards.

## 6. Checklist before delivery
1. Correct family for the document type.
2. PDF with embedded fonts for anything sent out.
3. Legal name on invoices and contracts.
4. Title line matches the audience.
