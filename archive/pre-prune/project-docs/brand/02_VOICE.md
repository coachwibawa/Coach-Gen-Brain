## 1. Brand platform
- **Who it is for.** People who keep trying, but never finish. Not people who lack effort: people whose effort keeps resetting.
- **What we believe.** Everyone is resourceful. You are not broken. When you are stuck, your power is pointed the wrong way or doubted, not missing. Gen is the editor who helps you rewrite your story, not the hero who saves you.
- **The promise.** "If you keep trying, but never finish, this is why."
- **The mechanism.** "You do not fail to make lasting change. You succeed at staying who you are."
- **The system.** The 5 Laws of Lasting Change, one per timeframe: Reset My Course, Reframe Past Regret, Resolve Present Friction, Reap Future Habits, Relax To Results.
- **Positioning (internal, not for publishing word for word).** For people who keep starting and never finishing, Coach Gen is the International Coach & Author whose 5 Laws of Lasting Change explain why the pattern repeats and how to end it, built from hundreds of hours of coaching in 5 countries.

## 4. Voice (always the same)
| Trait | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Plain and concrete | "The crush who laughed. The interview that said they would call back." | Abstract nouns, big words, "leverage your potential" |
| Calm and certain | "Before you can move forward, you have to stop moving backward. That's what Law 1 does." | Hype, countdowns, "Transform your life in 30 days", "Stop X. Start Y." |
| On your side | "Lazy people don't run a business, a side job, and three more projects." | Pity, diagnosis, "broken", "fix you", labels for the reader |
| From me, and true | "I hear the same 3 stories from almost everyone I coach." | "We", "our team", "my clients", invented claims, bragging about being first |
The examples on the left are his approved lines (Language Rules and Posting Rules).

## 6. Vocabulary
**Always, exactly as written:**
- Lasting Change. The 5 Laws of Lasting Change. Law names: Reset My Course, Reframe Past Regret, Resolve Present Friction, Reap Future Habits, Relax To Results.
- 3 Stages of Consistency: Consistent Effort, Momentum Engine, Effortless Result. Energy Audit.
- "keep trying, but never finish" (the "but" is load-bearing).
- "someone I coach", "the people I coach", "the person across from me".
- Inside The Introvert. Lasting Change Letters. Coach Gen. Genison Wibawa.

**Never (proposed from his rules, approve or strike):**
- "my clients", "we", "our team", "Coach Wibawa".
- "broken", "fix you", "lazy" as a label for the reader.
- Hype and genre clichés: "transform your life", "unlock your potential", "level up", "game-changer", "journey", "hack", "guru", "secret".
- Health labels aimed at the reader: "your anxiety", "your depression", "your trauma".
- Borrowed scare statistics ("unemployable") without a source in the same view.
- The ™ symbol in running copy. Product names stand on their own.

**Mechanics:** British spelling (realise, colour, behaviour), numbers as digits, "everyday" as one word, Oxford comma, no em dashes, no semicolons, no emojis, sign-off "Coach Gen". All from the Language Rules.

## 9. Checklist for any public copy

2. Voice traits hold. Tone fits the channel.
3. No word from the never list. British spelling, digits.
4. Every claim is true and on record.
5. Sentence-level check against the Language Rules checklist.
