## 2. AI images (Nano Banana, Gemini)
- Minimalist always: one object, lots of empty space, at most a word or two of text. Every extra detail is another chance for AI mistakes ("slop").
- No AI-made people presented as real people, clients or Gen.
- Instagram look: silhouettes and floating cut-out objects over a flat brand background.
- Meta's "AI label" toggle: Gen's decision every time, never the agent's.

## 3. Prompt template (fill the brackets)
"Minimalist image, one [object] in the [center or lower third], flat [Abyss #01131C or Charcoal #2B2B2B] background, [Aqua #72CCD0 or Gold #C8B796] as the only accent color, soft side light, lots of empty space, no text, no people, square corners, no gradients, [4:5 or 1:1 or 9:16]."
Add text afterwards in the template, not in the prompt.

## 4. Checklist
1. One object, empty space.
2. Palette colors only.
3. No AI-made people.
4. Text added in the template, on a panel.
