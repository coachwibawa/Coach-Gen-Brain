## 9. Caption skeletons
- **Teach (carousel or Reel):** a line the reader says to themselves, in quotes. The pattern behind it. The Law it belongs to. One call to action.
- **Thought:** one idea, one or two sentences, no call to action or the soft one.
- **Author:** a short excerpt, then "From Lasting Change Letters" or "From the book I'm writing".
- **Client story:** follow the LinkedIn and Facebook rules in the Posting Rules.

## 10. Ad copy pattern (Meta and LinkedIn)
1. Open with the reader's own self-talk, in quotes. Example (placeholder): "Monday again. This time I'll stick to it."
2. Name the pattern, not the person. Example: If you keep trying, but never finish, this is why.
3. One offer, one action. Example: Take the free diagnostic and find the Law that keeps you restarting.
Limits: primary text about 125 characters before "see more", headline about 40 characters.

## 11. Inquiry replies (WhatsApp and DM)
Ask before telling. No pressure, no discounts to close.
- **First reply:** Thanks for reaching out. Before I share anything, may I ask 2 things? What made you message me now, and what have you already tried?
- **When they ask the price first:** Happy to share. So I point you to the right program, what would you want to be different 3 months from now?
- **Booking:** Let's find a time. Here is my calendar: [link]. If none of these work, tell me 2 times that do.
- **Not a fit:** Thank you for being open with me. From what you shared, I don't think I'm the right coach for this. [name or resource] would fit better.
Sign-off on every reply: Coach Gen.
