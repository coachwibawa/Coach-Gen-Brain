## 5. How each Reel is built (Sinek style, from the Reel Style Guide)

- Starts mid-thought on the hook. No intro, no title card.
- Two shots from the one 4K camera: wide (as filmed) and close (about 1.6x, face centered). Switch at sentence breaks, every 5 to 15 s, which reads as two cameras.
- Captions: Bebas Neue, uppercase, white with soft shadow, 2 to 5 words per chunk, timed to each word, lower middle of the frame.
- Optional opening question box for the first 8 to 10 s (black box, top), as in the Sinek Oprah clip. Example for R1: "WHY ARE YOU NOT MOVING FORWARD?"
- Audio +12 dB. Dead air over 0.6 s trimmed inside the Reels.
- Coach Gen logo small in a corner the whole time (needs the logo file).
- Export 1080 x 1920, about -14 LUFS.
