# Tool Audit

Every tool in the experimental draft, checked against one standard, so each Law ends up with tools of the same quality.

## The standard (7 checks)

A tool passes a check when the chapter text alone lets the reader do it, without you in the room.

1. **One job.** It does one clear thing for this Law.
2. **Steps.** The reader can follow it alone, step by step.
3. **Time.** It says how long it takes, or how often. A micro tool fits in about 5 minutes.
4. **Output.** It leaves something the reader can see: a list, a mark, a number, a sentence.
5. **Shown.** A real person's result or filled-in example sits next to it.
6. **When.** It says when to use it (a trigger or a moment).
7. **Source.** Your own, client-tested, or borrowed and credited.

**Signature tool:** passes 6 or 7. **Micro tool:** passes at least 1, 2, 4. **Prompt:** a single question. Useful, but it is not a tool on its own.

## Spread rule

- Each Law chapter gets **one signature tool** and **one or two micro tools**.
- **Each format once per chapter at most.** A question list counts as one format.
- **From Within You never repeats a tool or question already in the chapter.**
- Across the book, the formats rotate (page, rounds, ladder, log, picture, script, rule), so no two Laws hand the reader the same kind of exercise.

## What it shows

1. **Law 1 is crowded, Law 4 is thin.** Law 1 has 7 tools and 3 of them score 6 or 7. Law 4 has 2, and neither passes as a signature tool. The promise ladder has no steps, no time and no example.
2. **Laws 1 to 3 each end with a question list.** Three chapters hand the reader the same format, and From Within You repeats those same questions. This is part of the "not enough variety" feeling.
3. **Almost no tool says how long it takes.** Only 3 of 17 do. Your Micro Tools format promises 5 minutes, so this is an easy win.
4. **Examples sit mostly in Law 1.** Laws 2 and 4 have tools with no filled-in example. The new material can fill these gaps (below).
5. **Law 5's tools are crammed into one paragraph.** Five tools at about 20 words each read as a list, not as tools. Pick one to be the signature tool and let it breathe.

## How to use this in the edit

When you rewrite a chapter, fill one row per tool: aim for one signature tool (6 or more) and one or two micro tools (passing 1, 2 and 4). Move a question list to From Within You only if the chapter does not already ask the same questions.
