## Rules for this work

- His voice: simple English, short sentences, vivid but not wordy. No em dashes, no semicolons, no emojis.
- Clients appear only under print names from the cast cards (Kin = David, Sri = Melina, Wayan = Bayu). Never real names. Follow the NEVER USE list: no health, money figures, organisation or club names in print, religious lines, or third parties' private stories.
- Kin's Purple Bird speech and its characters are Kin's, not Gen's. Don't use them.
- Stories marked "check he is happy to print it" (F-85, A-23, F-94) need his OK before going on stage.
- Options always as a numbered list. "ok" from him means go ahead.
- Empowering, never pitying. He is the editor of the story, not the hero.
