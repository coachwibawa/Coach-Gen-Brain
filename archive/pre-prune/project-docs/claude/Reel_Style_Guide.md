# Reel Style Guide

Version 1, 2026-10-21. Built from 13 reference Reels Coach Gen saved (4 Dan Koe, 4 Hiroaki Kato, 5 Simon Sinek). This is a working reference: add and annotate, never shorten.

How it was made: frames every 2 seconds from each Reel, cut times measured by scene detection, captions read from frames. Feel and music were not judged here. That is Coach Gen's call in review.

## Quick pick: which style for which post

| Post type | Style | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A thought, a belief, a reframe (Myth vs Reality, Identity Shifts) | Dan Koe | Calm, one idea, words carry it |
| Behind the scenes, events, Coach Gen as a person, announcements | Hiro | Warm, moving, playful, shows the man |
| Teaching a Law, Client Stories, Diagnostic Questions, talk and podcast clips | Sinek | Credible, expert in conversation |

## Shared rules (all three styles)

- Vertical 1080x1920. All 13 references are 720x1280, so Instagram does not need more.
- Loudness: all 13 sit at about -14 LUFS. Export ours to the same so they don't sound quieter in the feed.
- Captions are short chunks, 2 to 5 words, one idea at a time. Never a full sentence block, except the Dan Koe opening card.
- Captions sit in the lower middle of the frame (about 60 to 75 percent down), never at the very bottom where Instagram's buttons cover them.
- Length: 25 to 75 seconds. Most are 35 to 60.

## Style 1: Dan Koe (thoughts)

References: R01 (55s), R02 (38s), R03 (37s, animated), R04 (25s).

**Structure**
1. Hook card, 5 to 6 seconds: pure black screen, only words. His voice already speaks over it. The words are the hook line itself ("If you don't have anyone to talk about your interests and passions with, start writing on the internet and you'll find them.").
2. Hard cut to one talking-head shot. Then no more cuts until the end. One take.
3. No outro, no call to action card. It just ends.

Measured: the cut from black to face lands at 5.1 s, 5.8 s and 6.2 s. After that, zero cuts.

**Look**
- Hook card: white serif text on black. Left-aligned block (R01) or one centred line that changes every 1 to 2 seconds (R02, R04). Small and quiet, not shouting.
- Talking head: dark room, dark plain T-shirt, mic in frame, soft side light, chest-up framing, face in top half.
- Captions: very small (about half the size of Sinek's), one line, 2 to 4 words, changing fast. White sans-serif (R04) or gold serif (R02). Placed at chest height, about 70 percent down.
- Variant (R03): no face at all. Thin white line drawings on black that move with the idea (dots, two circles, waves), tiny uppercase labels. Book-cover white card at start and end.

**Feel:** quiet, serious, one idea, no tricks. The viewer is pulled in by the words, not by editing.

**What Coach Gen needs to film:** one calm talking-head take, dark background, dark shirt. Plus the hook line written out.

**Concat settings to start from**
- Hook card: background black, title font serif, fontSize 0.03, white, left-aligned, maxWidth 0.75, no background box, no shadow.
- Captions: fontSize 0.022, fontWeight 500, white or #D4A64A (gold), offsetY 0.2, no box.

## Style 2: Hiro (public figure persona)

References: R05 (24s, guest clip on a podcast), R06 (31s, event announcement), R07 (57s, travel vlog), R09 (61s, store visit).

**Structure**
1. Starts already moving: a greeting to camera ("Konnichiwa", "Hiroaki Kato di sini") or a fun intro effect (R06 starts with his face inside an old TV).
2. Selfie talk, walking, handheld.
3. Second half turns into quick B-roll: food, places, products, people, each shot about 2 seconds (R07 cuts at 30, 31.6, 32, 34.7, 36.7, 39, 40.8, 42.6, 44.4...).
4. Ends with an invite: "See you at...", "Ikutin aku", "Jangan lama-lama".

Measured pace: R05 (podcast clip) cuts every 1 second, 22 cuts in 24 s. Vlogs: talk section with few cuts, then B-roll every 2 seconds.

**Look**
- Captions: bold, uppercase, heavy condensed font, white with dark outline. One word in each chunk is highlighted (yellow text, or a coloured box behind the word). 2 to 4 words per chunk, Indonesian.
- Placed at chest height, about 70 to 75 percent down.
- Pop-in stickers and graphics: flags, emoji, a big "SAA IKOU!!" sticker, Halloween pumpkin, meme cut-ins (R05 cuts to anime and memes to land jokes).
- Bright, real places, natural light. Branded T-shirt with his own logo in almost every Reel.
- Colour frame/border (pink) on some cut-ins.

**Feel:** friendly, fun, "come with me". He is the brand. Japanese words mixed into Indonesian as a signature.

**What Coach Gen needs to film:** selfie clips talking to camera on location, plus 5 to 10 short B-roll clips (2 to 4 seconds each) of the place, people, food, details. Wear branded shirt if possible.

**Concat settings to start from**
- Captions: font heavy condensed (closest built-in to be tested), fontWeight 900, fontSize 0.03, white, strokeWidth 0.004 black, offsetY 0.25. Highlight word: separate title in #FFD400 or with a background box.
- Cut-ins: 1 to 2 seconds each.

## Style 3: Sinek (professional)

References: R08 (36s), R10 (75s), R11 (118s), R12 (59s), R13 (70s).

**Structure**
1. Starts mid-thought, on the strongest line. No intro. ("One of the biggest blind spots of founders is...", "I preach service...")
2. Clip from a longer talk, podcast or stage interview.
3. Cuts only when the camera angle or speaker changes, every 5 to 20 seconds. R12 is one single shot for 54 s.
4. Ends on the conclusion line, sometimes a short brand end card (R12: "Leaderful" logo, 2 seconds).

**Look**
- Captions: bold uppercase condensed font (Bebas Neue style), white with soft shadow, 2 to 5 words, one line. R10 uses colour per speaker: orange for him, blue for the host.
- R08 uses small serif captions instead (white, lower case), so a calmer version also exists.
- R11 has a black box with the big question at the top for the first 10 seconds, then no captions at all.
- Split screen with two speakers stacked (R10, R08): one on top, one on bottom, caption on the line between them.
- Show or channel logo stays in a corner the whole time.
- Real setting: stage chair, studio couch, mic in view. Looks like a professional event.

**Feel:** expert in a real conversation. Credible because someone invited him to speak.

**What Coach Gen needs to film:** a recorded session, talk, workshop or podcast-style conversation (Zoom recordings and live sessions count). A second person asking questions makes it stronger.

**Concat settings to start from**
- Captions: font bold condensed, fontWeight 800, fontSize 0.032, white, shadow on, offsetY 0.15 (single shot) or 0 (split screen, on the middle line). Speaker colours: #FF6A13 and #3FA9F5.
- Opening question box: background #000000, fontSize 0.03, offsetY -0.3, first 8 to 10 seconds.
- Logo corner: Coach Gen logo, small, top right, whole length.

## Decisions, 2026-10-21 (answers to the open items above)

- **Purpose of the references:** the Reels are a guide for presentation only (structure, pace, captions, look). The content is always Coach Gen's own.
- **Caption language:** follows the content. Spoken in Indonesian, captions in Indonesian. Spoken in English, captions in English. Not tied to the style.
- **Fonts (Claude's call, tested in Concat 0.2.8 on his Mac):**
  - Dan Koe style: **Baskerville** regular (weight 400). Hook card white. Captions white or gold #D4A64A.
  - Hiro style: **Anton**, uppercase, white with black outline (strokeWidth 0.004), key word in yellow #FFD400.
  - Sinek style: **Bebas Neue**, uppercase, white with shadow. Speaker colours #FF6A13 and #3FA9F5 for split screen.
  - Font test result: Bebas Neue, Anton, Impact, Avenir Next Condensed, Futura, Hanken Grotesk, Cabinet Grotesk, Georgia and Baskerville all render. EB Garamond and Instrument Serif are NOT installed and fall back to a plain sans font with no warning, so always check a preview frame after changing a font.
- **Transcription test:** Whisper (speech to text) installs here, but its model download is blocked by this workspace's network allowlist (huggingface.co returns 403). Other model sources are blocked too. Not solved yet. See the chat of 2026-10-21 for the options.
- **Transcription test, update (same day): SOLVED.** Coach Gen allowed huggingface.co and *.hf.co in the network setting. Whisper "small" model now runs here: his 20 s test memo was transcribed in 8 seconds, language detected (English), with a time for every word. Captions in Concat are now built from his real words, timed to his voice. Tech note: decode audio with ffmpeg to 16 kHz mono first, then pass it to Whisper (the direct file open fails on a library version mismatch).
- **Shadow glitch:** the faint light streak beside title boxes seen in the first trial is gone with shadow off. Keep shadow off unless a style needs it, and check a preview frame.
