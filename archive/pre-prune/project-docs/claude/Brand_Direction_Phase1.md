# Coach Gen Brand Direction, Phase 1: The Case

28 Oct 2026. Status: proposal, waiting on Gen's answers to section 6.
Role taken: a design director hired to replace work made piece by piece by interns and tools. Each decision states what changes, why it is better, and the risk.

## 1. Audit: what the current site and content actually say

Read on 28 Oct 2026: coachwibawa.com home, /about, /framework, /products, plus the CSS and fonts the site loads.

1. **Three names, one person.** "Coach Wibawa" (site header, page titles, domain, Instagram), "Coach Gen" (logo, Facebook, sign-off), "Genison Wibawa" (book cover). The site header still shows the old fingerprint C, not the new maze G.
2. **Wrong positioning.** Site title and hero: "The Introvert's Executive Coach". Footer: "Public Speaking Coach for Introverts". Your decided line is International Coach & Author. Your flagship, the 5 Laws of Lasting Change and the book, appears nowhere on the site.
3. **Too many named offers.** One home page names: Introversion Leverage, 6C of Public Speaking, Engineer of Influence, Introverts Mindset Mastery, Inside the Introvert Podcast, Three Transformation Keys, Inner Circle, Anchor Protocol, "Transform your life in 30 days". The About page adds "Engineered Framework". A visitor cannot say what you do in one sentence.
4. **"We" voice hides the asset.** About page: "The Engineers Behind The Coach", "our founder", "we earned". Page author tag: "Coach Wibawa Team". Your name does not appear on the About page. You are a solo coach and author. Clients buy you.
5. **Credential mismatch.** Site says CPC. Your records say ICF Associate Certified Coach (ACC). ICF is the credential international buyers recognise.
6. **Testimonials (corrected 28 Oct after Gen's answer).** All five (Wayan Perdana, Julie, Mira K., Dan Sato, Gurun P.) are real clients with permission. They stay. One follow-up: "Dan Sato" is also the name of Composite C in the workbook cast. A real client sharing a name with a composite can link him to stories that are not his. Rename Composite C.
7. **Borrowed statistics.** "Deemed unemployable", "salary increase", "$xB annual US cost" all come from one journal, use US data for a Jakarta and Singapore audience, and show as 0% until the counters animate. Big claims from a thin source lower trust with exactly the readers you want.
8. **Broken links.** /framework and /products sit in the menu and return 404.
9. **Template fingerprints.** The site loads 5 font families (Manrope, Libre Baskerville, PT Serif, Oswald, Outfit) and uses 1. The CSS still holds unused default "sidebar" tokens, a 135 degree navy-to-teal gradient and a glow shadow. These come with the starter template, not from a design decision.
10. **Outside the site, no system.** Reels use Bebas Neue, Anton and Baskerville with yellow, orange and bright blue highlights, and a text "COACH GEN" corner mark. Instagram slides from the Coachinator have their own look. Handles differ: @coachwibawa, @officialcoachgen, @askcoachgen.

**What works and stays:** the navy and teal family (it maps straight onto the kit's Deep Water and Aqua), the hero portrait, the idea "the problem is the method, not your personality", the ROI calculator as a tool, the real testimonials (Wayan Perdana), past-client logos (if permitted), and an assessment as the front door.

## 2. Stress test of my earlier plan

The earlier plan: 13 chapters (brand core, logo, color, type, layout, imagery, graphics, video, platforms, captions, series, print, rules), every platform, delivered as a Brand Book, a Design System, a file kit and skill updates. Where it breaks:

1. **It started at the logo, not the strategy.** A guide that codifies three names and ten offers only makes the confusion consistent. Fix: a new Chapter 0, brand architecture (names, master line, offer ladder), decided before any visual.
2. **It was written for a human designer.** Your designers are AI tools: Antigravity builds the site, Claude skills cut video and write captions and posts, the Coachinator makes slides. AI follows specs, not mood boards. Fix: the main output is a spec (tokens, rules, templates) that every skill reads. The pretty Brand Book is the human view of the same file.
3. **Rules without templates drift.** The intern problem was never a missing rule. Each piece started from a blank page. Fix: a closed set of master templates covers about 90% of output. A new format needs a template before it ships.
4. **"Gold and blue equally important" breaks on any single page.** Two equal palettes read as two companies. Fix: equal in status, different in job (D6).
5. **Too big to finish in one push.** 13 chapters at once, with usage limits, risks a half-built guide. Fix: ship in phases, each one usable on its own (section 5).
6. **It treated the website as one platform among many.** The site is the hub every post points to, and today it sells the wrong positioning. Fix: the site brief moves up to Phase 3.
7. **Platform-first spreads thin.** Fix: design for formats, not platforms. Six formats (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9, A4, web) cover every platform through one mapping table.
8. **Missing pieces.** No contrast checks (Deep Gold on Ivory fails for body text, see D6), no photo direction, and no link to the existing Language Rules. Fix: add all three, and point to the Language Rules instead of copying them.

## 3. Decisions and the case for each

**D1. Two names, two jobs.** Genison Wibawa is the author and the face. Coach Gen is the company brand and the logo. "Coach Wibawa" retires from everything visible. The domain and old handles stay and redirect.
- Why better: authors who build international brands publish under their full name (James Clear, Carol Dweck, Simon Sinek). Your book cover already says Genison Wibawa. "Coach Wibawa" matches neither the logo nor the book.
- Risk: current followers know "Coach Wibawa". Bridge with "Coach Gen (Genison Wibawa)" in bios for about 3 months.

**D2. One master story.** Master line: International Coach & Author. Master promise, from the book: "If you keep trying, but never finish, this is why." The 5 Laws of Lasting Change leads the site. Public Speaking (6C) and Introverts (ILA) become two specialty tracks under it, as your own overview already describes them.
- Why better: the book, the framework, 250 coaching hours, 5 countries and 3 languages are the assets nobody can copy, and none of them are on the site. "Introvert's executive coach" competes with hundreds of speaking coaches. The 5 Laws is yours alone. Every Reel, post and article then sells one thing.
- Risk: introvert followers may feel the focus moved. The Introverts track and Inside The Introvert keep them served.

**D3. One front door, an abundant catalog (revised 28 Oct per Gen).** The front door is a new 5 Laws diagnostic. The 6C, ILA and every other program stay featured, Tony Robbins style, so the range itself signals credibility.
- How the design makes abundance work: one hero offer per page, then a catalog where every offer uses the same card template (name, one-line promise, who it is for, one button), grouped under the 5 Laws master brand. Abundance reads as credibility when it is organised and as noise when every offer has its own look. The current site has the offers but not the shared structure.

**D4. "I", not "we".** Every page in first person, with your name and face.
- Why better: you are the product. "We" reads as an agency and hides your hours, your story and your book, which is also written in "I".

**D5. Real proof only.** Real quotes with permission, real logos with permission, and your own numbers: ICF ACC, 250+ coaching hours, clients in 5 countries, coaching in English, Indonesian and Japanese. Borrowed US statistics come out unless properly cited.
- Why better: your audience checks things. One quote that feels made up discredits the real ones. Your own numbers are stronger than borrowed ones.

**D6. Color roles.** Blue is the base system: Abyss, Deep Water, Aqua, Mist. Gold is the author and premium layer: the book, speaking, Lasting Change Letters, paid program documents, the International title. Black and white cover print limits and partner co-branding. One colorway per piece. Never gold and Aqua in the same composition.
- Why better: continuity, since the site is already navy and teal. Blue reads as calm and clear, which fits structure and an analytical audience. Gold kept rare stays premium. If everything is gold, nothing is.
- Contrast checks (WCAG): Aqua on Abyss 10.1:1, Deep Water on Mist 8.8:1, Gold on Charcoal 7.2:1, all pass for body text. Deep Gold on Ivory 3.2:1 passes for large headings only. Rule: in gold-on-light pieces, body text is Charcoal and gold is for headings and accents.

**D7. Two typefaces.** Montserrat for headings, labels, UI and video captions (the logo's own font). Libre Baskerville for reading: articles, book excerpts, quotes and thought-style Reels. Manrope, Oswald, Outfit, PT Serif, Bebas Neue and Anton retire.
- Why better: headings in the logo font make every page sign itself. A serif for long reading tells people "author". Libre Baskerville is already on the site and in your thought Reels, so this keeps what works. Two fonts means no AI tool ever has to choose.
- Risk: Bebas Neue is condensed and fits more words per line. At 2 to 5 words per chunk, Montserrat ExtraBold fits.

**D8. Captions in brand colors.** White Montserrat ExtraBold, caps, thin black stroke, one highlight word per chunk in Aqua (blue pieces) or Gold (gold pieces). Same position rules as now.
- Why better: your own site says the old advice "feels performative". Yellow and orange captions are the look of hype creators. Brand-color highlights make a Reel recognisable as yours in the feed before your face shows.
- Risk: loud yellow may stop more thumbs. Test: 3 Reels in each style, compare 3-second hold rate.

**D9. Shapes follow the logo.** Square corners (0 to 4 px radius), straight rules, everything on a grid. No gradients, no glows, no diagonals, no speed lines. One motif: the maze channel of the G, used as dividers, frames and progress lines (5 Laws = 5 steps).
- Why better: the logo says "structure". Rounded cards, glows and a diagonal gradient say "template". A layout that matches the mark is the difference between designed and decorated.

**D10. Photo direction.** Real you only: coaching, writing, speaking, thinking. Warm natural light, dark backgrounds that sit on Abyss or Charcoal. No stock, no AI-made people. One shoot day with a short list: portrait, writing at a desk, coaching (client from behind), on stage, holding the book.
- Why better: the hero portrait is the best thing on the current site. A person brand with one good photo runs out within a month.

**D11. Templates first.** A closed set of master templates: Reel (Thought, Persona, Professional), carousel 4:5, quote card 4:5, story 9:16, YouTube thumbnail 16:9, LinkedIn image, article header, newsletter email, slide deck, A4 document, plus the finished business card and name tag.
- Why better: it fixes the actual intern problem, the blank page.

**D12. One source of truth.** One BRAND-GUIDE.md and one tokens file, in the project and on the Mac, read by every skill (autopilot-video-edit, post-to-socials, coach-gen-writer) and by every Antigravity and Coachinator brief.
- Why better: a rule changes once and every tool follows.
