# Device mix: the reference books vs the experimental book (21 Oct 2026)

How much of each book is the author's own story, other people's stories, research, explanation, and tools.

## Method
- Reference books: 50 passages of about 110 words, spread evenly through the body text of each book (front matter, notes and index left out), each labelled by hand. A passage that mixes devices is split (for example half own story, half explanation). Share = percent of passages. Margin: roughly plus or minus 5 to 7 points per category.
- Our book: every paragraph of the Introduction and Chapters 1 to 7, minus headings, Chapter Summaries and From Within You, labelled by the concept it carries (from the Book Map), weighted by words.

## Results (percent of the text)

| Device | Atomic Habits | Mindset | Courage to Be Disliked | Our book (all) | Our Law chapters (Ch3 to Ch7) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Author's own story | 8 | 8 | 2 | 13 | 8 to 11 |
| Other people's stories and cases | 14 | 44 | 4 (+7 the youth's own life) | 31 | 25 to 49 |
| Research and data | 9 | 13 | 0 | 3 | 0 to 6 |
| Explanation, argument, analogy | 50 | 22 | 87 | 44 | 31 to 44 |
| Tools and exercises | 11 | 12 | 0 | 10 | 9 to 18 |
| Summaries, cheat sheets | 7 | 0 | 0 | (left out) | |

## What it says
- **Own stories are not the gap.** Clear and Dweck each spend about 8% on their own lives. Our Law chapters already sit at 8 to 11%. More own stories would not, by themselves, fix "not enough variety".
- **Breadth of people is the gap.** Dweck's 44% of stories comes from about 22 different people in 50 passages (Christopher Reeve, Jack Welch, Mia Hamm, John Wooden, a boy called Jimmy, a couple in therapy). Clear uses about 13 different people and teams in his sample. Our 31% comes mostly from the same three clients, plus about 7 others across the whole book.
- **Research is thin.** 3% against 9 to 13% in Clear and Dweck. Each of them backs most big ideas with a study or a number. Courage has none, because it is a dialogue built on argument.
- **Explanation is in the normal range.** 44%, between Mindset (22%) and Atomic Habits (50%).

So the variety to add is more different people (famous, historical, ordinary, other fields) and more research, not more of the same cast and not much more of his own life.
