## Structural principles

- [stated] The 5 Laws are differentiated by timeframe, not emotion
- [stated] Emotional-word overlap across Law Step Snapshots (for example "inspired" appearing in both Law 2 and Law 3) is structurally fine and should never be flagged as a collision problem
- [stated] Do not invent unstated context to justify a differentiation concern; check the locked structure first

## Word-choice rationale (load-bearing)

- [stated] The "but" in the tagline is deliberate: it creates tension versus a neutral listing
- [stated] "Reap" implies ease, framing habits as harvest
- [stated] "My" in Law 1 is intentional: it is the one law that must come from inside the client
- [stated] "To" in Law 5 implies cause-and-effect rather than immersion

## Coined terms (Law 1)

- [stated] "Energy Audit" (coined 19 Oct 2026) names the whole Law 1 process: map where thought and energy go, by feeling (the thought map, its first step), then write it down to purge unneeded ones (the Productivity Purge page). Energy is not a goal yet, so the audit only shows where you are.
- [stated] The thought map and the page with its marks are flagship exercises: allude to them and build hype, the way James Clear does

## Law 1 vs Law 2 boundary (20 Oct 2026)

- [stated] Law 1 is recognizing your left foot is on the brake and attempting to move it. Law 2 (regret) is when the left foot keeps coming back even after you swore you never would, sometimes harder, sometimes gone for a week then back. Some feet don't come back: sometimes Law 1 is enough
- [stated] Law 1's hidden reward guards a test that hasn't happened yet; Law 2's guards a failure that already happened (the wall is time)
- [stated] Full form of his thesis line: "If I don't try and fail, I'm lazy. If I try and fail, I'm incompetent."
